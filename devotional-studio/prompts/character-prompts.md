# Character prompts for ChatGPT image generation ("Deepa Chitra" style)

How to use:
1. Open a NEW ChatGPT chat for each character. Paste the STYLE BLOCK first, then the character prompt.
2. Generate the CHARACTER SHEET first (landscape). Pick the best one.
3. In the same chat, ask for the FULL-BODY HERO shot ("use the same character from the sheet").
4. Download both PNGs at full resolution and upload them here. They become the master
   reference for redrawing each character in code and keeping every video on-model.

---

## STYLE BLOCK (paste before every prompt)

Art style: "Deepa Chitra", a lamp-lit Indian temple painting. Kerala mural painting traditions blended
with clean modern 2D illustration. Bold dark-brown outlines (not black), flat colour fills with soft
painted shading, and shining gold-leaf details like Tanjore painting on every ornament. Elegant,
graceful, devotional and serene. Large almond-shaped lotus-petal eyes with long kajal flicks, thin
arched eyebrows, a long straight nose and small full lips. Calm, divine expressions, never cartoonish
or chibi, and never a realistic photo or 3D render. Colour palette: deep vermilion red, peacock and
malachite green, indigo blue, ochre, warm skin tones, rich gold. Warm oil-lamp lighting with a soft
golden rim light. Clean, readable silhouettes that can be animated. No text, no watermark, no
signature, no logo, no frame.

## 1. TULASI DEVI (Vrinda): CHARACTER SHEET

Create a professional character turnaround sheet for animation, landscape 3:2, on a plain warm
cream/parchment background with soft shadows under the feet.

Character: Vrinda (Tulasi Devi), a young, graceful, devoted married woman from ancient India, about 22
years old. Slim, elegant build with Kerala-mural proportions. Gentle, kind, serene face.

Show, in one row, the SAME character at the SAME scale, full body, head to bare feet:
front view, three-quarter view, side profile view, back view.
Below them, a smaller row: 4 head close-ups with expressions (peaceful prayer with eyes half-closed,
gentle smile, shocked realisation with eyes wide, fierce divine anger as she curses). Next to those:
her hands in anjali mudra (palms pressed together in prayer), plus a small Tulasi (holy basil) sprig
held in her palms.

Design details (keep EXACTLY the same in every view):
- Skin: warm golden-ochre / light honey-brown with soft peach shading.
- Hair: long, thick, jet-black. Centre parting with a thin line of red sindoor (she is married). Hair
  gathered in a low bun at the back of the head, encircled by a ring of white jasmine flowers. Two
  strings of jasmine fall behind the ears to the chest.
- Forehead: round vermilion-red bindi. A gold maang tikka chain along the parting with a small
  diamond-shaped gold pendant with a red stone. A thin gold head chain (nethi chutti) along the hairline.
- Ears: gold bell-shaped jhumka earrings with tiny pearl drops.
- Blouse: ONE single emerald / peacock-green silk blouse with elbow-length sleeves, a rounded
  sweetheart neckline and gold zari borders at the neckline, sleeve ends and lower hem. The sleeves
  must be continuous with the bodice (no seam, no separate pieces).
- Saree: deep vermilion red silk with a small gold dot-and-diamond butta pattern. Broad gold border
  with a green wave and dot motif. Pleats in front. The pallu drapes over her LEFT shoulder and falls
  down her back to the knees.
- Waist: gold waist belt (oddiyanam) with a central ruby.
- Jewellery: a gold choker with green and red stones, a long gold bead necklace with a diamond-shaped
  gold pendant with a ruby, three gold bangles plus one red glass bangle on each wrist, and gold armlets
  hidden under the sleeves.
- Feet: bare feet with red alta borders, silver anklets and toe rings.
- Overall colour signature: GREEN blouse + RED saree + GOLD jewellery.

Label nothing. No text.

## 2. LORD VISHNU: CHARACTER SHEET

Create a professional character turnaround sheet for animation, landscape 3:2, on a plain warm
cream/parchment background with soft shadows under the feet.

Character: Lord Vishnu, the preserver. Youthful, eternally calm, compassionate and majestic, with a
gentle half-smile and half-lowered eyes. Kerala-mural / South Indian temple proportions.

Show, in one row, the SAME character at the SAME scale, full body, crown tip to bare feet:
front view, three-quarter view, side profile view, back view.
Below them, a smaller row: 4 head close-ups (serene and compassionate, gentle smile, sorrowful as he
accepts the curse, eyes closed in meditation). Next to those, close-up studies of his four hands:
1) upper right hand balancing the Sudarshana Chakra on the raised index finger,
2) upper left hand holding the white Panchajanya conch (shankha),
3) lower right hand in abhaya mudra (open palm facing viewer, fingers up, blessing),
4) lower left hand in varada mudra (palm facing viewer, fingers pointing down, giving).

Design details (keep EXACTLY the same in every view):
- Skin: deep luminous blue (like a clear evening sky / blue lotus), with soft lighter-blue highlights
  and indigo shading. Smooth skin.
- Body: graceful and slim with broad but gentle shoulders and a SMOOTH, soft chest. NO visible
  muscles, NO abs, NO six-pack, not a bodybuilder. A slim waist and a small navel only. Four arms
  that come naturally from the shoulders (the back pair slightly behind the front pair). Arms smooth
  and rounded with natural elbows.
- Crown: an ornate gold kirita mukuta. Tall but broad and richly layered, NOT a narrow plain cone.
  Tiers of rubies and emeralds, a large central ruby on the band, lotus-bud finial on top, and small
  flame-shaped side ornaments.
- Hair: long black curls falling behind the shoulders.
- Forehead: Vaishnava urdhva pundra tilak (white U-shaped namam with a red central line).
- Ears: gold makara kundala (fish-shaped) earrings.
- Chest: the Kaustubha gem (a large ruby pendant) on a layered gold necklace; a small Srivatsa mark on
  the right chest; a white sacred thread (yajnopavita) across the chest from the left shoulder to the
  right hip.
- Vanamala: a long garland of green tulasi leaves and small flowers from the shoulders to the knees.
- Arms: gold armlets (keyura) on all four upper arms and gold bangles on all four wrists.
- Lower garment: bright golden-yellow silk pitambara dhoti with pleats, a gold and red embroidered
  waistband with a central jewel, and a light silk sash.
- Feet: bare feet with gold anklets.
- Weapons and attributes: Sudarshana Chakra (gold, spiked, glowing) and white shankha with gold caps.
  Optional: gada (golden mace) resting beside him in the side views only.
- Overall colour signature: BLUE skin + YELLOW silk + GOLD ornaments + GREEN garland.

Label nothing. No text.

## Negative / avoid (add at the end if results drift)
Avoid: muscular body, six-pack, abs, bodybuilder chest, extra or missing fingers, extra arms, fused
hands, separate sleeve pieces, seams on the blouse, cartoon chibi proportions, anime style,
photorealism, 3D render, horror, dark scary mood, text, watermark, signature, logo, cropped feet,
cropped crown.

## Consistency tips
- Keep each character in its own chat so ChatGPT remembers the design.
- For a new pose, say: "Same character as the sheet above, same costume and jewellery, now [pose]."
- If hands come out wrong, ask for "a close-up of only the hands, same character" and pick the best.
