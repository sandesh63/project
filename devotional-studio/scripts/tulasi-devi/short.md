# Tulasi Devi — YouTube Short (≈55 sec)

Goal: hook in 2 seconds, build the mystery, STOP before the answer, send viewers to the long video.
Pace: ~2.6 words/sec, short sentences, pauses at "…". Visual notes reuse the preview scenes.

**Title ideas:** "Bhagwan Vishnu ko PATTHAR kisne banaya? 😱" · "भगवान विष्णु को पत्थर बनने का श्राप किसने दिया?"

## HINGLISH

|---|---|---|

| 3–8 s | Eyes open, look at camera | Na kisi rishi ne… na kisi devta ne. Ek pativrata stree ne — **Vrinda** ne. |
| 8–17 s | Temple wide, golden arch, lamps | Vrinda ki bhakti itni shaktishaali thi… ki uske pati Jalandhar ko swayam **Mahadev** bhi harā nahi pa rahe the. |
| 17–27 s | Moonlit doorway, silhouette | Phir ek raat… Vrinda ke dwaar par uska pati aaya. Wahi chehra… wahi aawaaz… |
| 27–33 s | Shadow grows four arms | Lekin deewar par padti uski parchhai ke… **chaar haath** the. |
| 33–40 s | Anger close-up, tear, red energy | Jab Vrinda ko sach pata chala… aankhon mein aansu the… aur hothon par ek shaap. |
| 40–47 s | Flash → Vishnu, eyes closed | *"Jaise tumne mera hriday patthar kar diya… waise hi tum bhi **patthar** ban jaoge!"* |
| 47–51 s | Turns into Shaligram | Aur Bhagwan ne… woh shaap chupchaap sweekar kar liya. |
| 51–55 s | Tulasi plant + title, "Full story" | Lekin kyun? Aur Vrinda… **Tulasi** kaise bani? Poori kahani dekhiye — link neeche hai. 🙏 |
